# Runbook: Incident Response

## Severity Levels

| Severity | Impact | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| SEV-1 | Full outage | 15 min |
| SEV-2 | Partial outage | 30 min |
| SEV-3 | Minor degradation | 2 hours |
| SEV-4 | No user impact | Next business day |

## Response Steps

1. Acknowledge alert in PagerDuty
2. Join #incident Slack channel
3. Assign Incident Commander
4. Assess affected services
5. Contain and remediate
6. Communicate status updates every 30 min
7. Resolve and schedule post-mortem
